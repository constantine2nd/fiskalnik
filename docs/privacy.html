Privacy policy

Fiskalnik (Chrome extension) · Last updated: 27 November 2026

In short

The extension reads a Serbian fiscal receipt only when you ask it to, and opens a Google Calendar event form filled in with that receipt, in your own browser. It stores nothing and sends nothing to the developer. There are no accounts, no analytics and no ads.

What the extension reads

  • A receipt page you have open. On suf.purs.gov.rs/v/… it adds the “Додај у календар” button. When you click it, the extension reads that page: merchant name, address and tax number, receipt number, date and time, cashier, items, totals, VAT and payment method.
  • An image you give it. When you click “Скенирај QR на овој картици”, it takes one picture of the tab you are looking at. You can also paste, choose or drop an image. The picture is searched for a QR code inside your browser and then discarded; it is not saved and not sent anywhere.

It does not read other pages, your browsing history, your calendar or your Google account, and it does nothing until you click one of its buttons.

What it stores

Nothing. The extension has no storage of its own and keeps no history of receipts. The warranty period you choose is not saved either. Once the popup or the tab is closed, the receipt is gone from the extension.

What it sends, and to whom

  • To the Tax Administration's portal (suf.purs.gov.rs): when a QR code contains a receipt link, the extension downloads that receipt page, just as if you opened the link yourself.
  • To Google Calendar (calendar.google.com): when you click “Додај у календар”, a new tab opens Google Calendar's “new event” form. The receipt details (the list above, the receipt link, and the warranty end date if you set one) are part of that link, so Google receives them in that request. The event is saved only if you press Save, in the Google account you are signed in to, and is then covered by Google's privacy policy. “Додај датум истека гаранције” opens a second form the same way.

No data is sent to the developer or to any other server, and there is no analytics or tracking code. QR codes that are not links to suf.purs.gov.rs are ignored.

Sharing and use

  • The data is not sold, shared with or transferred to third parties, other than being handed to Google Calendar at your request, as described above.
  • It is used only to create your calendar event, not for any other purpose.
  • It is not used to determine creditworthiness or for lending purposes.

Permissions

  • activeTab: to take one picture of the current tab when you click “Скенирај QR на овој картици”.
  • suf.purs.gov.rs: to show the “Додај у календар” button on receipt pages and to download the receipt a scanned QR code points to.
